# Final Project: Research Track (assignment text)

Investigate a specific, open research question related to AI. Identify the question, explain your approach, and present evidence that helps answer it. Papers discussed in class can serve as starting points.

Possible directions include:

* **Evaluations or benchmarks:** Develop tasks and assessment criteria for a domain you care about, then evaluate agents or models on them.
* **Agent methods:** Investigate a planning, memory, or tool-use method and compare it with a suitable baseline, such as a general-purpose agent working on the same task.
* **Human-agent interaction:** Explore how people provide input to agents or receive and use their outputs. Or think about other ways of interaction beyond just text.
* **Agent simulation:** Study behavior that emerges when agents interact in a controlled environment, e.g., a specific scenario.
* **Learning from experience:** Investigate whether an agent can improve on a specific task through experience or feedback.
* **Questions from papers:** Investigate an unanswered question raised by a paper discussed in class.

Make the scope concrete. For example, "a self-evolving agent" leaves open what the agent will learn, how it will learn, and how you will measure improvement. A focused question makes your results easier to interpret.

## Where our project fits
Mainly **Learning from experience** + **Agent methods** (self-feedback / memory vs. baselines), with a small **Evaluation/benchmark** component (the SVG country-shape test suite).
